[h1]Comprehensive Statistics[/h1]

Open the statistics window with the inventory button or a custom hotkey to view your character career, current-world activity and selected world-wide records.

[h2]Statistics[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Blocks:[/b] placement and removal of blocks, walls, furniture, circuits and liquids, organized into four subtabs
[*][b]Items:[/b] pickups, use and drops
[*][b]Ammunition:[/b] expected consumption, actual consumption and consumption rate
[*][b]Interactions:[/b] NPC dialogue, facilities and mechanisms
[*][b]Kills:[/b] final kills, assists and current-world NPC deaths
[*][b]Economy:[/b] trading, nurse healing, reforging, coins and special currencies
[*][b]Fishing:[/b] three subtabs for catches by liquid, cast outcomes by rod, and bait consumption with expected/actual amounts and consumption rate
[*][b]Movement:[/b] distance by movement method and source
[*][b]Survival:[/b] deaths and actual life lost, grouped by source
[/list]

[h2]Usage[/h2]
[list]
[*]Search, filter vanilla/modded content, sort columns and fold groups, with separate page views saved per character and world
[*]Supports single-player, host-and-play and dedicated servers, with English and Chinese interfaces
[*]Optionally supports ImproveGame 1.8.2 and later; hover over ? on Blocks for available tools. Both sides must update this mod for multiplayer
[*]Records activity only after the mod is enabled; reset, import/export and cross-character merging are unavailable
[/list]
