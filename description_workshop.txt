[h1]综合统计[/h1]

你是否好奇自己放置过多少物块、走过多远，或者哪一种伤害夺走了你最多生命？

综合统计提供一个统一的游戏内窗口，用于查看角色生涯、角色在当前世界中的活动，以及少量当前世界公共事件。你可以通过物品栏旁的按钮打开窗口，也可以设置自定义快捷键。

[h2]可以统计什么？[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]物块：[/b]物块、背景墙、家具、多格物体、电线和制动器的放置与拆除
[*][b]物品：[/b]物品的拾取、使用与丢弃
[*][b]弹药：[/b]按具体弹药记录应耗数量、实际消耗与消耗率
[*][b]交互：[/b]NPC 对话、对话按钮、家具、增益站、容器、晶塔、钟和机关
[*][b]击杀：[/b]角色的最终击杀与协助击杀，以及各类 NPC 在当前世界中的实际死亡次数
[*][b]经济：[/b]买入、卖出、护士治疗、重铸、金币收入、支出、净流量与特殊货币
[*][b]钓鱼：[/b]抛竿、成功渔获、空竿或中断、钓到的物品与 NPC，以及液体类型
[*][b]鱼饵：[/b]按具体鱼饵记录应耗数量、实际消耗与消耗率
[*][b]移动：[/b]步行、跳跃、飞行、钩爪、绳索、坐骑、矿车及其他方式的移动距离
[*][b]生存：[/b]死亡次数与实际掉血量，并按直接伤害、持续伤害和其他来源分类
[/list]

[h2]适合查看长期积累的大量记录[/h2]

[list]
[*]在“角色生涯”和“角色当前世界”两种范围之间切换
[*]搜索统计条目，并筛选原版或模组内容
[*]点击任意表格列进行排序，展开或折叠分组明细
[*]按角色、世界和统计页面分别保存分组展开状态
[*]在单人、主机联机和专用服务器中继续记录自己的角色统计
[*]提供统计数据的模组被移除后，仍以保存的内容键保留记录；重新安装后可恢复名称和图标
[*]支持简体中文与英文
[/list]

[h2]注意事项[/h2]

[list]
[*]本模组只会从安装并启用后开始记录，无法还原此前游戏过程中的历史数据。
[*]角色统计属于个人数据并随角色保存。多人游戏中只能查看自己的角色统计，不能查看其他玩家的完整个人记录。
[*]当前世界公共统计仅包含受支持的世界级记录，例如 NPC 实际死亡次数；本模组不是服务器排行榜。
[*]本模组支持原版内容，以及遵循 Terraria/tModLoader 标准机制的模组内容。高度自定义的模组机制可能显示为“未知/未分类”；无法可靠测量时也可能不记录。
[*]当前版本暂不提供统计重置、导出、导入、合并或跨角色共享功能。
[/list]

[h1]Comprehensive Statistics[/h1]

Ever wondered how many blocks you have placed, how far you have travelled, or what has taken the most life from you?

Comprehensive Statistics adds one in-game window for exploring detailed records from your character career, your activity in the current world, and selected current-world events. Open it from the inventory button or assign a configurable hotkey.

[h2]What can it track?[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Blocks:[/b] blocks, walls, furniture, multi-tile objects, wires, and actuators placed or removed
[*][b]Items:[/b] items picked up, used, and discarded
[*][b]Ammunition:[/b] expected and actual consumption by ammo type, including conservation rate
[*][b]Interactions:[/b] NPC conversations, dialogue buttons, furniture, buff stations, containers, pylons, clocks, and mechanisms
[*][b]Kills:[/b] your final kills and assists, alongside each NPC type's actual deaths in the current world
[*][b]Economy:[/b] purchases, sales, nurse healing, reforging, coin income, spending, net flow, and special currencies
[*][b]Fishing:[/b] casts, catches, empty or interrupted casts, caught items and NPCs, and liquid type
[*][b]Bait:[/b] bait selected and consumed, with conservation rate
[*][b]Movement:[/b] distance travelled on foot, by jump, flight, grapple, rope, mount, minecart, and other methods
[*][b]Survival:[/b] deaths and actual life lost, broken down by direct damage, damage-over-time effects, and other sources
[/list]

[h2]Made for large, long-running records[/h2]

[list]
[*]Switch between character-career and character-in-current-world statistics
[*]Search records and filter vanilla or modded content
[*]Sort any table column and expand or collapse grouped details
[*]Keep separate group expansion states for each character, world, and statistics page
[*]Use the same character statistics in single-player, host-and-play, and dedicated-server sessions
[*]View missing mod content by its saved content key; reinstalling that mod restores its name and icon
[*]Play in English or Simplified Chinese
[/list]

[h2]Please note[/h2]

[list]
[*]Recording starts only after this mod is installed and enabled. Activity from earlier play sessions cannot be reconstructed.
[*]Character statistics are personal and saved with that character. In multiplayer, you can view your own character data, not the complete personal records of other players.
[*]Current-world public statistics are limited to supported world-wide records, such as NPC deaths; they are not a server leaderboard.
[*]Vanilla content and mod content using standard Terraria/tModLoader behavior are supported. Highly custom mod mechanics may appear as unknown or unclassified, or may be omitted when they cannot be measured reliably.
[*]The current version does not provide statistics reset, export, import, merging, or cross-character sharing.
[/list]
