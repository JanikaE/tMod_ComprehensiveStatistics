[h1]Comprehensive Statistics / 综合统计[/h1]

Comprehensive Statistics records detailed character activity and selected world-wide events from the moment the mod is enabled.

[list]
[*]Blocks, walls, wires, actuators, furniture, and multi-tile objects
[*]Item pickup, use, discard, ammunition consumption, and bait consumption
[*]NPC interactions, final kills, assists, and current-world NPC deaths
[*]Purchases, sales, nurse healing, reforging, and tax collection
[*]Fishing results, movement distance, deaths, life lost, and damage sources
[*]Character-career and per-world views with search, source filters, sorting, and persistent group expansion
[*]Single-player, host-and-play, and dedicated-server support
[*]English and Simplified Chinese localization
[/list]

Statistics begin when the mod is enabled; events from earlier play sessions cannot be reconstructed.

[h1]综合统计[/h1]

综合统计从启用模组后开始记录详细的角色活动，以及少量由当前世界统一保存的公共事件。

[list]
[*]物块、背景墙、电线、制动器、家具与多格物体
[*]物品拾取、使用、丢弃，以及弹药和鱼饵消耗
[*]NPC 交互、最终击杀、协助击杀和当前世界 NPC 实际死亡
[*]买入、卖出、护士治疗、重铸和税款收集
[*]钓鱼结果、移动距离、死亡、掉血与伤害来源
[*]角色生涯与分世界查看，支持搜索、来源筛选、排序和持久化折叠状态
[*]支持单人、主机联机和专用服务器
[*]支持英文与简体中文
[/list]

本模组只能统计启用之后发生的行为，无法还原此前的历史数据。
